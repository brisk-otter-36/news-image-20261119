# Sources — the news of Thursday, 19 November 2026

Every story on the front page was reported on 19 November 2026. Notes below
name the reports each headline rests on.

## Lead — Trump, Xi & Putin hold first-ever three-way summit (Shenzhen)

USA Today (19 Nov 2026) reported the first-ever trilateral talks between the
United States, China and Russia on the sidelines of the APEC forum in
Shenzhen on Nov. 19, lasting 90 minutes. The Times' live coverage and AP
reporting (carried in the Bangladesh Chronicle's morning briefing for
Thursday, November 19, 2026) describe the joint declaration that followed:
a pledge of dialogue on reducing nuclear risks, UN and ICC reform, and
stable energy flows.

## UN Court: Myanmar — genocide verdict (The Hague)

Just Security's "Early Edition: November 19, 2026" reported that the U.N.
International Court of Justice ruled that day that Myanmar committed
state-sponsored genocide, the first ruling of its kind by the highest U.N.
court. Algenom's news edition for Thursday, November 19, 2026 confirms the
ICJ delivered its judgment that Thursday in the case The Gambia filed in
2019 over Myanmar's 2017 crackdown on Rohingya Muslims.

## Ukraine strikes Crimean Bridge (Ukraine war)

Just Security (19 Nov 2026) reported Russia's statement that Ukraine
attacked the Crimean Bridge for the third time that week. UBStandard's
world page for Thursday, November 19, 2026 headlined the bridge partially
collapsing in what Zelensky called an "unprecedented" seven-stage strike,
and The Times reported Putin vowing a "much more painful" response.

## Israeli tanks push into central Gaza (Middle East)

UBStandard (Thursday, November 19, 2026) reported Israeli tanks pushing
into central Gaza beyond the ceasefire line near Nuseirat, with residents
describing artillery fire. Just Security's Early Edition for the same day
reported the IDF had launched a rare operation in part of central Gaza
after issuing evacuation orders to residents.

## Cyclone Akvan nears India coast (severe weather)

Algenom's November 19 edition, citing the India Meteorological Department
via The Week and IANS, reported Cyclone Akvan was expected to cross the
north Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha coast by noon on Friday,
November 20, as a severe cyclonic storm with sustained winds of
90–100 km/h gusting to 110 km/h.
